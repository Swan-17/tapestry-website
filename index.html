About the Show

Experience the epic story of the 1066 Norman Conquest as you have never seen—or heard—it before. The Bayeux Tapestry Musical weaves dramatic live music, historical storytelling and theatrical performance into the legendary scenes embroidered across the famous tapestry.

Join us in Guernsey for an unforgettable evening celebrating our island's remarkable medieval connection to Normandy and British history.
