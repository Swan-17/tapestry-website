About the Show

Experience the epic tale of the 1066 Norman Conquest like never before! The Bayeux Tapestry Musical weaves dramatic live music, historical storytelling, and theatrical performance to recreate the legendary scenes embroidered in the famous tapestry.

Join us in Guernsey for an unforgettable evening celebrating our island's unique medieval connection to Normandy and British history.
